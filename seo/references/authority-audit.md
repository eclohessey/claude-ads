# Authority & Off-Site Audit — 15 Checks (AU01-AU15)

Category weight: 20%. Links still dominate Google rankings; third-party mentions
dominate what AI answers say about you. Requires a backlink data source
(Search Console links report at minimum; Ahrefs/Semrush/Moz if available).

## Link Profile Health (AU01-AU06)

| ID | Check | Severity | Pass Criteria |
|----|-------|----------|---------------|
| AU01 | Referring-domain count competitive for the niche | High | Within range of the sites ranking top-5 for target terms |
| AU02 | Referring domains growing, not decaying | High | Net new domains per quarter is positive |
| AU03 | Link sources are topically relevant | High | Majority from the same industry or adjacent topics |
| AU04 | Anchor text distribution looks natural | Medium | Mostly brand and URL anchors; exact-match commercial anchors <10% |
| AU05 | No paid, PBN, or link-scheme footprint | Critical | No purchased placements, sitewide footer links, or reciprocal link farms |
| AU06 | Links point deep, not only at the homepage | Medium | Money and content pages each earn links |

## Technical Link Hygiene (AU07-AU09)

| ID | Check | Severity | Pass Criteria |
|----|-------|----------|---------------|
| AU07 | Backlinks resolve to live 200 pages | High | No high-value links pointing at 404s — reclaim via 301 |
| AU08 | Redirected links consolidated | Medium | Old domains/URLs 301 to current equivalents, one hop |
| AU09 | Unlinked brand mentions converted | Medium | Sites mentioning the brand without linking are contacted |

## Brand & Entity Signals (AU10-AU12)

| ID | Check | Severity | Pass Criteria |
|----|-------|----------|---------------|
| AU10 | Branded search volume growing | High | The strongest correlate of durable rankings; check Search Console brand queries |
| AU11 | Consistent NAP across directories | Medium | Name/address/phone identical everywhere; Critical for local businesses |
| AU12 | Owned profiles on the platforms AI answers cite | High | LinkedIn, Crunchbase, G2/Capterra, YouTube, relevant subreddits |

## Earned Coverage (AU13-AU15)

| ID | Check | Severity | Pass Criteria |
|----|-------|----------|---------------|
| AU13 | A repeatable link-earning asset exists | High | Original data, tool, benchmark, or research others cite |
| AU14 | Digital PR or podcast/guest cadence active | Medium | Ongoing, not a one-off campaign |
| AU15 | Competitor link gaps identified and worked | Medium | Domains linking to ≥2 competitors but not to you, with an outreach list |

## What Actually Earns Links Now

Ranked by observed return, best first:

1. **Original data** — a survey, a benchmark, an analysis of your own usage data.
   One good dataset out-earns fifty guest posts.
2. **Free tools and calculators** — durable, compounding, linked from forums.
3. **Being quoted as an expert** — podcasts, journalist requests, industry panels.
4. **Definitive reference pages** — the page people link to when they need to
   explain a concept in your category.
5. **Genuine community participation** — slow, but it is also what feeds AE21.

## What To Avoid

- Buying links, link exchanges, and PBNs (AU05) — detection has outpaced the tactic.
- Mass guest-post outreach on unrelated sites; relevance gates the value (AU03).
- Directory blasts. A handful of real industry directories help; volume does not.
- Chasing Domain Authority as a target. It is a third-party estimate, not a
  Google metric — report it as context, never as a goal.
